# Contracts

Contracts are posted on town quest boards. Each contract has a finite supply and a finite reward budget, so it can be taken only while supply and budget remain.

- A gathering contract asks you to deliver items, such as 3 Wolf Mint. Items gathered before you accept the contract still count.
- A hunting contract asks you to defeat enemies, such as 2 wolves in Mossway. Only victories after you accept the contract count.

Return to the contract's town while idle and claim it before it expires. The reward belongs to the job you had when you accepted the contract. Claiming again does not pay twice.

Your private plan is your own goal list. It does not accept contracts, track their progress or claim rewards.
